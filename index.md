# Collageup Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-11-23
Developer: elmenshawyapps

## Summary
Collageup edits your photos on your device. We do not run a server, we do not have accounts, and we do not collect, upload or sell your photos or personal data.

## What the app accesses
- **Photos you choose.** The photo picker gives the app only the pictures you select. They are read and edited on your device and are never uploaded by us.
- **Saving to your gallery.** When you tap Save, the app writes your finished slides to your photo library (Photos on iOS, Gallery on Android). This needs your permission.
- **Sharing.** When you tap Share, your slides are handed to the app you choose (Instagram, TikTok, Messages…). What that app does with them is covered by its own privacy policy.

## What we collect
Nothing. We have no analytics, no advertising SDK and no crash-reporting service in the app. We do not know who you are or what you create.

## Purchases
Subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are handled entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We receive no payment details. The app stores on your device only whether Pro is active.

## Data on your device
Settings (language, whether you finished onboarding, Pro status) are stored locally. Temporary export files are deleted after saving or sharing. Uninstalling the app removes all app data.

## Fonts
All fonts are bundled inside the app (SIL Open Font License). No font is downloaded.

## Children
Collageup is not directed to children under 13 and collects no personal data from anyone.

## Changes
If this policy changes we will update the date above and publish the new version at the same address.
